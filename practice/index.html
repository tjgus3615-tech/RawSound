01. Background & Purpose

  • Overcoming Communication Barriers: Experiencing difficulty understanding diverse regional English accents during study abroad/travel despite knowing standard English.
  • Learning 'Raw' English: Moving beyond textbook English to embrace real-world accents spoken across different cultures worldwide.
  • Building Confidence: Listening to diverse stories in authentic accents, mastering them to build real-world communication confidence.

02. Goals & Target Users

Project Goals

Understand & speak diverse accents/intonations, significantly boosting real-world listening, speaking, and confidence.

Target Users
  • Students preparing for study abroad or exchange programs
  • Learners aiming to improve or diversify their English pronunciation
  • Actors and voice artists needing to perform specific regional accents
  • Global English users seeking real-life listening comprehension

03. Key & Additional Features

Accent TTS Engine

Select any country or regional accent to convert words & sentences into authentic audio.

AI Shadowing & Feedback

Record your voice to receive instant AI accuracy evaluation and custom feedback.

Gamified Accent Quiz

Listen to audio clips in different accents and guess the country or dialect in fun stages.

Slang Dictionary & Speed

Learn cultural slang & idioms with adjustable audio playback speed controls.

04. Differentiation

Unlike traditional apps limited to standard US/UK/AU accents, RawSound covers Europe, East Asia, Middle East, Africa, and specific regional dialects within countries (e.g., Liverpool Scouse, Glasgow, etc.).

05. Benchmark & Vision (Speak)

Role Model Company: 'Speak'
Inspired by Speak's effective implementation of AI conversational features, RawSound aims to build an accent-focused AI engine to break down real-world communication barriers worldwide.
